# 03 · Timecoded Storyboard

Master: 1920 × 1080 · 30 fps · 1800 frames · 60.000 s · H.264 · stereo 48 kHz.
Music grid: **90 BPM**, beat = 0.6667 s, bar = 2.6667 s. Every cut and accent is on that grid.

Transitions are centred on the scene boundary (the outgoing and incoming scenes are both
rendered and blended), so scenes overlap and hand over rather than cut.

| Boundary | Style | Window |
|---|---|---|
| 01 → 02 | push up (90 px, soft edge) | 0.72 s |
| 02 → 03 | 9-column wipe, staggered | 0.80 s |
| 03 → 04 | 9-column wipe, staggered | 0.78 s |
| 04 → 05 | dissolve + emerald exposure bump | 0.74 s |
| 05 → 06 | 9-column wipe, staggered | 0.80 s |
| 06 → 07 | dissolve + exposure bump | 0.95 s |
| 07 → 08 | dissolve + exposure bump | 0.85 s |

## SCENE 04 — THE PORTFOLIO SHOWCASE · 0:19 – 0:29

Three beats of 3.333 s (5 bars each). Layout deliberately alternates so no beat repeats the last.

| Beat | t (s) | Headline | Asset | Layout |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | 19.00–22.33 | `LOGO DESIGN` | `w02` Cargo Services | plate right (×1345), type left |
| 02 | 22.33–25.67 | `SOCIAL MEDIA DESIGN` | `w01` Restaurant | plate left (×600), type right |
| 03 | 25.67–29.00 | `SHORT-FORM VIDEO ADS` | `w03` Travel Agency | plate centre, headline below |

Per beat:
- **0.00–0.95 s** — plate enters on a **26° diagonal mask wipe** (150 px soft edge) with scale 1.07 → 1.00 and a 46 px counter-slide; soft drop shadow; 1 px hairline frame; emerald registration marks snap in with overshoot at +0.30 s.
- **0.35 s** — headline clip-rises word by word (0.075 s stagger).
- **1.05 s** — emerald rule grows to 190/200 px.
- **1.35 s** — mono caption `SELECTED WORK — <REAL VERTICAL>`.
- **Background** — the *next* project appears as a blurred, 30 % depth layer, so the sequence reads as one continuous space.
- **Exit** — last 0.60 s: slides out 46 px as the next beat slides in (cross-fading hand-off).

Grid: two vertical hairlines at columns 4 and 8 sweep in at the scene start and persist —
the Swiss system made visible. Footer: `PORTFOLIO — SELECTED PROJECTS`.

## Continuity devices

1. **The outline `NAIK` wordmark** bookends the film (Scene 01 draw-on → Scene 08 draw-on).
2. **The dot field, architectural frame and corner ticks** persist across all eight scenes,
   so the film reads as one space rather than eight templates.
3. **The emerald rule** recurs as the punctuation between every headline and its caption.
4. **The colour swatches** close the film using your own banner's motif.
