# 04 · Sound Design, Audio & Licensing

## The constraint, stated plainly

This environment has **no access to a paid or free music library** — no Artlist, no
Epidemic Sound, no Pixabay, no Free Music Archive. It cannot download stock audio at all.

So the score was **composed and synthesised from scratch in code** (`src/audio.py`).
Every sound in the film is generated by maths — sine waves, filtered noise and envelopes.
There are no samples, no loops, no downloads.

That is not a compromise, it is the better answer here:

- **Original** — you own it outright, nothing to clear, nothing to attribute, no licence
  that expires, no Content ID claim on YouTube or Instagram.
- **Beat-locked** — the score is written on the *same 90 BPM grid the picture is cut on*,
  which is something you can almost never achieve with a stock track. Every transition,
  impact and reveal lands on a frame boundary because both were built to one clock.
- **Shaped to the film** — it drops out for the philosophy scene and rebuilds for the
  end card, because it was written for those moments.

File: `output/naik-brand-film-score.wav` (48 kHz, 16-bit stereo, 60.000 s).

## Musical design

**Tempo** 90 BPM · beat = 0.6667 s · bar = 2.6667 s · 90 beats total.
**Key** A minor 7 (A · C · E · G) — dark, unresolved, leaves room for picture.
**Structure** mirrors the eight scenes:

| Scene | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 01 Signature | 0:00–0:06 | Sub-bass swell, pad entering slowly. Sparse ticks on beats 3 and 6. A riser under the name reveal. |
| 02 Identity | 0:06–0:13 | First impact on the cut. Kick on beats 13 and 17; off-beat hats from beat 12. |
| 03 Experience | 0:13–0:19 | Deeper impact, sustained sub, hats on the off-beats. |
| 04 Showcase | 0:19–0:29 | Full pulse: kick every two beats, hats on every beat, an impact + whoosh on each of the three portfolio beats. |
| 05 Motion | 0:29–0:37 | Riser and whoosh as the print lifts; impact on "MOTION". |
| 06 Technology | 0:37–0:43 | Half-beat ticking layer (1.5–2.9 kHz, alternating L/R) over a steady kick. |
| 07 Philosophy | 0:43–0:52 | **Drums drop out entirely.** Pad and sub only — the quietest passage in the film, so the line can be read. |
| 08 Signature | 0:52–1:00 | Impact at the cut, pulse rebuilds, sustained chord, final sub hit at 58.5 s, resolve. |

**Measured levels:** peak −0.72 dBFS, RMS −17.5 dBFS, crest ≈ 18 dB (sparse and
cinematic by design). Master chain is tanh soft-clip → peak normalise to 0.92 →
4 ms fade in → 0.35 s fade out matched to the picture's resolve to black.

### Sound palette

| Element | Synthesis |
|---|---|
| Pad | six detuned sines (±0.35 Hz) per chord note, slow attack/release, gentle tremolo |
| Sub | sine with an exponential pitch drop (e.g. 70 → 38 Hz) |
| Kick | pitch-swept sine 118 → 44 Hz + a 20 ms noise click |
| Hat | noise burst, high-passed one-pole, 45 ms decay |
| Tick | 35 ms sine blip at 1.5–2.9 kHz |
| Whoosh | noise through a **swept** one-pole filter (chunked, cross-faded) |
| Riser | noise through a rising high-pass with a long crescendo |
| Impact | sub drop + filtered noise crash + a 4-tap tail |

Deliberately restrained: there is no riser-and-impact on every cut, no gaming-montage
whoosh spam. The film has **six** impacts in sixty seconds.

## If you would rather use licensed music

You asked for both, so you have them:

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `output/naik-brand-film-60s.mp4` | with the synthesised score |
| `output/naik-brand-film-60s-silent.mp4` | **no audio** — drop your own track in |

**To add your own music** (no re-render of the picture, takes seconds):

```bash
ffmpeg -i output/naik-brand-film-60s-silent.mp4 -i your-track.wav \
       -map 0:v:0 -map 1:a:0 -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 256k -ar 48000 -ac 2 \
       -shortest output/naik-brand-film-60s-yourmusic.mp4
```

Pick a track at **90 BPM** (or 180) so the cuts stay on the beat, and trim it to 60.0 s.
If you use a track at a different tempo, the picture will still work — the cuts are
already evenly spaced — but the accents will stop lining up.

### Where to get properly licensed music
- **Free:** Free Music Archive (`freemusicarchive.org`), Pixabay Music, YouTube Audio
  Library, Incompetech (CC BY — attribution required).
- **Paid, safest for client work:** Artlist, Epidemic Sound, Musicbed.

**Check the licence for commercial/client use specifically** — "free for YouTube" and
"free for commercial use" are different grants, and CC BY requires visible attribution.
